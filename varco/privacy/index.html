Privacy policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Varco is built to keep as little about you as possible. It works offline, everything you add stays on your device, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

This policy explains what the Varco app and this website collect, why, and the choices you have. "We" means CiaoBeppe, the maker of Varco. Questions: varco-support@ciaobeppe.com.

Without an account

Varco Lite and Varco Complete both work without an account. Your verbs, vocabulary, corrections, notes, settings and practice statistics are saved only on your device. We don't receive any of it.

Pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech; we don't receive what is spoken.

With an account (Varco Complete)

If you create an account to back up and sync, we store:

  • Your email address and password. The password is protected with one-way encryption; nobody, including us, can read it.
  • A copy of your Varco data: your custom verbs and words with their definitions, notes and categories; your corrections and other changes to any verb or word; what you hide or delete, so it stays hidden or deleted on every device; and the time of each change.
  • Practice statistics: your totals, across devices, for each tense and word (with a device identifier), and a day-by-day history kept for 13 months.
  • Settings: your choice about helping improve Varco, and when you last reset your statistics.
  • Subscription status: whether your Varco Complete subscription is active and when it ends. We never see your payment details.

We use this to run your account, back up and sync your data, and send account-related emails (sign-in codes, password resets, account deletion codes). We access it only when needed to run the service, fix a problem, keep it secure, or answer a request from you.

Helping improve Varco (off unless you turn it on)

In Menu → Account you can opt in to help improve Varco. It is off by default. If you opt in, we study your corrections to verb forms, the verbs and words you add, and your practice statistics, together with other learners' and only in aggregate, to find errors in Varco's conjugations and common trouble spots for students. We never use your notes, your email or anything saved only on your device. You can opt out at any time.

Who handles data for us

We use a small number of service providers, each only for the job listed and bound to protect the data:

  • A cloud database and sign-in service: stores account data and handles sign-in (United States).
  • An email delivery service: sends account emails, such as sign-in codes (United States).
  • Apple: handles App Store purchases and subscriptions. Apple's privacy policy applies to your payment.
  • A subscription-status service: tells the app whether your subscription is active. It receives a purchase record from Apple and a random identifier, never your name or email.
  • Our email provider: receives and stores messages sent to varco-support@ciaobeppe.com.

We don't sell, rent or share your personal information, and Varco has no ads, trackers or third-party analytics.

Where it is stored, and for how long

Account data is stored in the United States. It is kept until you delete your account, except the day-by-day practice history, which is deleted after 13 months. When you delete your account, it and everything it owns are removed from our database at once; copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within a short period. Support emails are kept as long as needed to help you.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in Menu → Account → Delete account, or ask us by email.
  • Correct your data in the app at any time.
  • Get a copy of your account data, or ask any question about it, by email.
  • Withdraw consent to the improvement opt-in at any time.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. We rely on our contract with you to run your account, on your consent for the improvement opt-in, and on our legitimate interest in keeping Varco secure and working. You may file a complaint with your data protection authority. If you are in California, note that we do not sell or share personal information.

Children

Varco is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. Children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA and UK) should create an account only with a parent's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

This website

ciaobeppe.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Like any website, its hosting service records visitors' IP addresses in its logs, for security. Its fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
