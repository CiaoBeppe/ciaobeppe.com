A pocket-sized Italian verb quizzer and a living, customizable dictionary. You choose what to learn; Varco helps you practice it. In bocca al lupo!

For iPhone and iPad. Coming soon to the App Store.

About Varco

Varco began as a personal project. As an Italian learner moving from Elementary into Intermediate levels, I found myself overwhelmed by new verb tenses, grammatical rules, and vocabulary arriving faster than I could absorb them. I tried every app I could find (flashcards, verb conjugators, guided language learning apps), but none let me choose what mattered to me. I wanted a tool that supported my formal classes, reinforced what I was learning, and let me build a personalized dictionary of words I actually used.

So, I built Varco: No gamification, no distractions, just a clean, focused companion for real Italian study. You choose what to learn; Varco helps you practice it.

What it does

  • Conjugation quizzes for every common tense, from Elementare to Avanzato, with the grammar rule one tap away.
  • Translation quizzes for verbs and for your own vocabulary.
  • Your own verbs and words: add, correct, hide or delete anything, starter list included.
  • Statistics that show what you know and what needs another look.
  • Works offline. Everything is saved on your device.

Two ways to use it

Varco Lite, free

  • Every quiz, at every level
  • All the starter verbs and vocabulary
  • 5 verbs and 5 words of your own
  • Statistics for Presente, Infinito and Vocabolario

Varco Complete, subscription

  • Unlimited verbs and words of your own
  • Full statistics for every tense
  • Pronunciation of words and verb forms
  • Extra quizzes, with new ones added over time
  • Optional backup and sync across devices
