About Varco

Varco began as a personal project. As an Italian learner moving from Elementary into Intermediate levels, I found myself overwhelmed by new verb tenses, grammatical rules, and vocabulary arriving faster than I could absorb them. I tried every app I could find (flashcards, verb conjugators, guided language learning apps), but none let me choose what mattered to me. I wanted a tool that supported my formal classes, reinforced what I was learning, and let me build a personalized dictionary of words I actually used.

So, I built Varco: No gamification, no distractions, just a clean, focused companion for real Italian study. You choose what to learn; Varco helps you practice it.
